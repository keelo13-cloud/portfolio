## n8n

### Twitter/X Thread Automation
- **Cut thread production from up to a full day to under 5 minutes.** The CEO just uploads a video explaining the play to Google Drive — the system writes the thread, brands the images, and publishes automatically.
- Replaced a slow manual process (screenshot the play, edit images, write copy, post through Hypefury) with a hands-off pipeline.
- Every thread is branded with the academy's logo, link, and slogan — reinforcing the brand and driving traffic on every post.
- Turned a one-a-day bottleneck into threads on demand, cross-posted to Facebook automatically to extend reach.
- Automatically quote-tweets the academy's clips on a steady drip, keeping both the academy's and the founder's accounts consistently active without any manual posting.

### Daily Task Feed + CEO Task DM
- **Brought every task into Discord — the team's main hub** — automatically pulling from ClickUp and posting a daily feed each morning, so no one has to leave Discord or dig through boards to know what's due.
- Sends the CEO a personal DM of his own priorities, keeping him on top of the day without asking anyone for a status update.
- Added a one-tap "Send for Review" button right in the feed — the team moves tasks forward without ever leaving Discord.
- Replaced manual check-ins and status chasing with an automatic feed, keeping the whole academy aligned with zero admin effort.

### Daily KPI Report from Custom Dashboard
- **Delivers the academy's key numbers to Discord every morning** at 8AM — sales, traffic, and channel performance in one automatic report, no spreadsheets or manual pulls.
- Built full marketing attribution behind it, so the CEO can see exactly which channels and campaigns are driving revenue — not just totals, but where the money actually comes from.
- Tracks upgrades from entry offers to the high-ticket membership, showing how leads move up the funnel.
- Turned scattered data across WooCommerce, GA4, and the site into one plain-view daily snapshot the whole team sees without lifting a finger.

### Notify Editor on New Video Upload
- **Alerts the video editor the moment a new file lands** in a specific Google Drive folder — pinged directly in Discord so nothing sits waiting to be noticed.
- Removed the back-and-forth of the CEO manually telling the editor a video is ready — the handoff now happens on its own.
- Keeps the content pipeline moving without anyone checking Drive or chasing updates, so editing starts sooner and videos ship faster.

### ClickUp EOD Report to Discord
- **Posts an end-of-day summary to Discord automatically** — a clear rundown of what got done and where things stand, delivered without anyone writing it up.
- Gives the CEO and team visibility into daily progress without status meetings or manual recaps.
- Closes out each day with an accountability snapshot in the team's main hub, keeping everyone aligned on what moved and what's still open.

### YouTube Thumbnail Generator Agent
- **Turns a single typed title in Discord into a finished thumbnail** — the editor just types the title and gets a ready-to-use image built to the CEO's style and preferences.
- Removed the design step from the editor's plate — no manual mockups, no waiting on a designer, no guessing the CEO's look.
- Keeps every thumbnail on-brand and consistent while producing them in seconds, so videos ship faster without a creative bottleneck.

### Meeting Minutes to Tasks (Fathom)
- **Turns every meeting into structured notes and action items automatically** — call transcripts become clean minutes posted to Discord and tasks created in ClickUp, with no one typing up recaps.
- Makes sure decisions and to-dos from meetings never slip through the cracks — action items are captured and assigned the moment the call ends.
- Saves the team the manual work of reviewing recordings, writing summaries, and creating tasks by hand, so everyone leaves a meeting with the work already queued up.

### Daily Content Research Agent
- **Delivers a trending-content briefing to Discord every morning** — pulls what's hot across multiple sources, analyzes it with AI, and posts a structured report the team can act on.
- Gives the CEO and creators fresh content ideas daily without anyone manually scrolling platforms or researching trends.
- Turns hours of scattered research into a ready-made report each morning, so the academy always knows what to make next.

## Zapier

### Multi-Platform Clip Distribution
- **Pushes every short clip to all social channels at once** — one new clip goes out to TikTok, Instagram, Twitter, and Facebook automatically, with zero manual reposting.
- Removed the tedious cycle of downloading, reformatting, and uploading the same clip to each platform one by one.
- Multiplied the academy's reach from a single piece of content — every clip works across every channel the moment it's ready.

### Email Support Automation
- **Handles incoming support emails automatically** — each message is analyzed for its topic and urgency, with a suggested reply generated and the ticket routed to the right person.
- Cut response times dramatically by removing manual sorting and triage — the team picks up already-organized tickets instead of digging through an inbox.
- Keeps customers from waiting, so support stays fast and consistent even as volume grows.

### Subscriber Update Notifications
- **Keeps subscribers in the loop automatically** — new videos, posts, updates, and live sessions trigger an email to the Mailchimp list without anyone hitting send.
- Removed the manual step of writing and blasting an announcement every time new content drops.
- Drives the audience back to fresh content the moment it's live, boosting views and engagement with no ongoing effort.

## GoHighLevel (GHL)

### Email Campaigns
- **Runs the academy's email marketing end to end** — designed and built responsive HTML/CSS campaigns that stay on-brand across every inbox and device.
- Gave the CEO a reliable channel to reach the full audience with polished, professional emails instead of plain, inconsistent sends.
- Keeps subscribers engaged and moving toward offers, turning the email list into a steady driver of traffic and sales.

### SMS Drip Campaign Automation
- **Nurtures leads automatically over SMS** — a timed sequence of texts goes out on its own, keeping the academy in front of prospects without anyone sending messages by hand.
- Reaches leads where they actually read — right on their phone — driving higher response than email alone.
- Keeps every lead warm and moving toward a purchase around the clock, with no manual follow-up from the team.

### Post-Purchase Email Automation
- **Welcomes every new customer automatically** — a sequence kicks off the moment someone buys, delivering onboarding, next steps, and what to expect without any manual sends.
- Gets customers into the product faster and reduces confusion right after purchase, when it matters most.
- Builds retention and sets up upsells on autopilot, turning a one-time buyer into an engaged member with zero ongoing effort from the team.

### AI Appointment Setter (Retell AI)
- **Calls and books leads automatically** — an AI voice agent phones free-course leads, has a natural conversation, and schedules them straight onto the calendar without a human dialing.
- Works a 4,000+ lead database at scale — following up with every lead consistently, something a manual team could never keep up with.
- Updates the CRM in real time and captures call details after each conversation, so the pipeline stays current on its own.
- Turns cold and idle leads into booked appointments around the clock, filling the calendar without adding headcount.

### Appointment Confirmation + Reminder
- **Makes sure booked leads actually show up** — confirmations and reminders go out automatically ahead of every appointment, with no one manually following up.
- Cuts no-shows by keeping the appointment top of mind, so the team's calendar time isn't wasted on empty slots.
- Runs entirely on its own for every booking, protecting the sales pipeline with zero manual effort.
